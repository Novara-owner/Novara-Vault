# Governance

English · [简体中文](GOVERNANCE.zh-CN.md)

Novara is a single-maintainer project. This page records how decisions are made and how the community can take part, so that expectations are written down rather than implied.

## Decision model

The project is **maintainer-led**. The maintainer holds final decision rights over the roadmap, the architecture, what enters a release, and this document. Day-to-day decisions are made directly; larger ones (a new feature area, a contract change, a release) are made deliberately and recorded — the release notes and the documentation carry the reasoning.

## How the community takes part

- **Issues** are open to everyone and are the primary channel for bug reports, questions and feature proposals. A proposal that survives discussion becomes a tracked issue, which is how the roadmap grows.
- **Pull requests** are welcome. They are reviewed by the maintainer; the review criteria are the ones stated in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) (a single concern, tests for new behaviour, the DCO sign-off) and the project conventions in [docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md).
- **Security reports** never go through public issues — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the private channel.

## Roles

The signing policy in [docs/CODE_SIGNING_POLICY.md](docs/CODE_SIGNING_POLICY.md) defines the project's formal roles — Authors, Reviewers and Approvers — and currently lists the maintainer for all three. The continuity arrangements around these roles are described in [CONTINUITY.md](CONTINUITY.md).

## Changes to this document

This document is updated by the maintainer. Substantive changes are made in their own commit with a dated note in the commit message, so the history of the governance rules is the repository history itself.
